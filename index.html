🧪 Active vs Passive Unit 6

Present & Past Simple · Active & Passive Voice

✨ Active vs Passive

🟢 ACTIVE VOICE — Present Simple

subject + V1 / V-s + object

The chef cooks the soup.

She makes cheese from milk.

🟢 ACTIVE VOICE — Past Simple

subject + V2 + object

Isaac Newton invented the cat door.

Marco Polo brought pasta to Italy.

🔵 PASSIVE VOICE — Present Simple

am / is / are + V3 (+ by ...)

The mixture is cooked in an oven.

Different liquids are added to the paste.

🔵 PASSIVE VOICE — Past Simple

was / were + V3 (+ by ...)

The boomerang was discovered in Poland.

Bagpipes weren't found in Europe first.

Ice cream and pasta were brought to Italy from China.

🔑 WHEN TO USE PASSIVE

We use the passive when the action is more important than who does it, or when we don't know who does it.

We use by to show who does the action:

The cat door was invented by Isaac Newton.

⚡ Tip: If you can answer "Who did it?" easily and it's important — use ACTIVE. If the action itself is the focus — use PASSIVE.
